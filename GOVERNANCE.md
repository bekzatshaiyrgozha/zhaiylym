# Governance and Ownership

Zhaiylym is a student project created for the Introduction to Open Source course (DPG Track) at Kazakh-British Technical University.

## Ownership

Copyright remains with the project's contributors, and their work is released under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).

## Decisions

Maintainers discuss changes in public issues and decide by agreement. A change to the license, data policy or governance needs agreement from all maintainers.
